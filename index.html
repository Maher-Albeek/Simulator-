RC-N1 setup and troubleshooting
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS or Linux).
  2. Switch the controller on. The drone can stay off.
  3. Plug a USB-C data cable into the port on the bottom of the controller, not the phone port on top.
  4. Press Connect RC-N1 and choose the DJI port. If two are listed, pick the one named “For Protocol”; if no stick data arrives, reconnect with the other.
